## Implementation Notes (2026-10-02)

- Angular 22 `unit-test` builder ships vitest 5.x, but `@vitest/coverage-v8` was missing/mismatched (3.2.7 from another workspace package), causing `fetchCache` crash
- Added `@vitest/coverage-v8@^5.0.0` to `apps/web/package.json`
- Configured `angular.json` test options with coverage enabled, reporters, 75% thresholds, and sensible excludes
